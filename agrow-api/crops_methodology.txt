FINAL CROP THRESHOLD METHODOLOGY
================================

Purpose
-------
This file documents the source hierarchy and decisions used in:

crops_gaez_ecocrop_doa_slope_with_pucuk_paku_final.csv

The dataset contains 64 Sabah crops and vegetables.

SOURCE HIERARCHY
================

1. FAO GAEZ Ecocrop Search
--------------------------
Source:
https://gaez.fao.org/pages/ecocrop-search

For 63 crops with a located GAEZ Ecocrop record, the temperature,
rainfall, soil-depth and pH values are taken from or reconciled against
the GAEZ-hosted FAO Ecocrop database.

Pucuk Paku (Diplazium esculentum) was not located as a complete Ecocrop
record. Its row therefore uses a documented provisional profile derived
from species-specific field studies, occurrence information and
conservative agronomic judgement.

2. Malaysia Department of Agriculture slope guidance
-----------------------------------------------------
Source:
https://www.doa.gov.my/doa/resources/aktiviti_sumber/sumber_awam/penerbitan/buku/garis_panduan_pembangunan_pertanian_tanah_bercerun_2025_v2.pdf

max_slope_pct is based on the crop groups and rounded terrain-class
boundaries published in the Malaysia Department of Agriculture's
Garis Panduan Pembangunan Pertanian di Tanah Bercerun, Edisi 2025.

The stored rounded boundaries are:

2 degrees  -> 4.0 percent
12 degrees -> 21.0 percent
20 degrees -> 36.0 percent
25 degrees -> 47.0 percent
30 degrees -> 58.0 percent

Pucuk Paku is treated as Group A, Sayuran:

max_slope_pct = 21.0

This is a Malaysian land-management limit rather than an absolute
physiological tolerance.

FIELD DEFINITIONS
=================

min_temp_limit
    Provisional absolute lower temperature boundary in degrees Celsius.

ideal_temp_min
    Lower boundary of the preferred or successfully cultivated
    temperature range.

ideal_temp_max
    Upper boundary of the preferred or successfully cultivated
    temperature range.

max_temp_limit
    Provisional absolute upper temperature boundary in degrees Celsius.

min_annual_rainfall
    Lower annual-rainfall value in millimetres used by the suitability
    model. Irrigation and continuous soil moisture must still be
    considered separately.

min_soil_depth_cm
    Minimum effective soil-depth class used by the model.

ideal_ph_min and ideal_ph_max
    Preferred soil-pH range used by the model.

max_slope_pct
    Upper rounded percentage boundary for the relevant DOA crop group.

Evidence and decisions by field
-------------------------------

ideal_temp_min = 25.00
ideal_temp_max = 34.00

Evidence level: moderate to high.

ECHO Asia successfully cultivated vegetable fern at a site where
temperatures were typically 25-30 degrees Celsius. A separate
peer-reviewed field experiment in southern Thailand cultivated it
during temperatures of 26-34 degrees Celsius. The combined observed
productive range, 25-34 degrees Celsius, is therefore stored as the
preferred range.

Sources:
https://www.echocommunity.org/en/resources/390f2f8b-9835-444e-bfed-6f96abe3b9ec
https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/9/2/259

min_temp_limit = 18.00
max_temp_limit = 36.00

Evidence level: provisional expert judgement.

No species-specific controlled experiment establishing absolute lethal
or physiological minimum and maximum temperatures was located. The
values 18 and 36 degrees Celsius bracket the documented productive
25-34 degree range while remaining conservative for a moisture-loving,
cold-sensitive tropical fern.

These are model thresholds, not experimentally proven biological
limits. They should be revised if controlled temperature-response data
becomes available.

min_annual_rainfall = 1200.0

Evidence level: low to moderate; occurrence-derived.

A documented occurrence of Diplazium esculentum was reported in an
environment receiving 1,200-1,400 mm annual rainfall. Field cultivation
at ECHO occurred under 1,700 mm and 2,297 mm annual rainfall, but
irrigation was supplied during dry periods. The lower documented
occurrence value, 1,200 mm, is used as a provisional minimum.

The species requires consistently moist soil. Annual rainfall alone is
not sufficient: irrigation, drainage, seasonality and proximity to
waterways remain important.

Sources:
https://indiabiodiversity.org/observation/show/1805836
https://www.echocommunity.org/en/resources/390f2f8b-9835-444e-bfed-6f96abe3b9ec
https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/8/0/8014
https://www.jircas.go.jp/en/database/thaivege/047

min_soil_depth_cm = 20

Evidence level: provisional expert judgement.

The peer-reviewed Thailand experiment used rhizomes approximately
10-15 cm long and prepared the field to a depth of 15 cm. NParks
describes the plant as having underground fibrous roots and rhizomes.
No controlled minimum effective rooting-depth threshold was located.

The database uses standardized lower boundaries for soil-depth classes.
The shallow class begins at 20 cm, so 20 cm is assigned as the
conservative model value.

Sources:
https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/9/2/259
https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/8/0/8014

ideal_ph_min = 4.6
ideal_ph_max = 6.5

Evidence level: mixed.

The peer-reviewed field experiment successfully cultivated the species
in strongly acidic soil, with recorded plot pH values from 4.60 to 4.97.
This directly supports the lower boundary.

The upper boundary of 6.5 is provisional. It extends the usable range
toward mildly acidic soil, consistent with general fern cultivation and
the species' preference for moist, fertile loam, but it was not
established by a species-specific pH-response experiment.

Sources:
https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/9/2/259
https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/8/0/8014

Shade and moisture limitation
-----------------------------
The threshold table has no columns for shade or soil-moisture regime,
but both are essential for Pucuk Paku.

The strongest cultivation results were obtained under substantial
partial shade, while unshaded cultivation showed poorer performance or
higher damage. The species is commonly associated with moist ground
near rivers, creeks and rice fields.

Sources:
https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/9/2/259
https://www.echocommunity.org/en/resources/390f2f8b-9835-444e-bfed-6f96abe3b9ec
https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/8/0/8014
https://www.jircas.go.jp/en/database/thaivege/047

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
========================

The Pucuk Paku values are suitable provisional inputs for a rule-based
Sabah crop-suitability prototype. They must not be described as official
FAO Ecocrop limits.

Classification of Pucuk Paku fields:

- Directly supported productive range:
  ideal_temp_min, ideal_temp_max

- Direct lower evidence plus provisional upper interpretation:
  ideal_ph_min, ideal_ph_max

- Occurrence-derived:
  min_annual_rainfall

- Expert-estimated because no exact threshold was located:
  min_temp_limit, max_temp_limit, min_soil_depth_cm

- Government land-management classification:
  max_slope_pct

The model should preferably expose a source/confidence note in its user
interface whenever it reports Pucuk Paku suitability.
